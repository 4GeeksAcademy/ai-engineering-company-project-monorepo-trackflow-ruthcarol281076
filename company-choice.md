# PROJECT DECISION RECORD

## My chosen project
- The project chosen for development during the Bootcamp is TRACKFLOW.
- Inventory logistics management, which supports the delivery of service to the end customer (last mile), who ultimately confirms whether they have received excellent service.
- Support for business clients—the companies or merchants that send the products—and management of those client relationships.
- Management of carrier companies to coordinate the logistics of deliveries and returns.

## TRACKFLOW project characteristics
- Logistics and physical operations — moving packages from a warehouse shelf to someone's door.
- Cross-border complexity — two countries, two languages, two regulatory environments, and two separate technology stacks that need to be unified.
- Data engineering in its most concrete form — visually compelling dashboards based on structured carrier performance metrics, SKU-level inventory, shipment event streams, and returns classification.
- Systems that operate 24/7 — the CX agent, tracking portal, and operations dashboard should always be available.

## My AI agent idea
- An AI agent that manages package arrivals and departures in inventory, then tracks packages until they reach the customer.
- It feeds data about arrivals and departures—the foundational steps of the logistics business—and subsequently provides data for the dashboard.
